# Code of Conduct

## Standards Community Ethos (IETF RFC 7154 & Contributor Covenant 2.1)

Participation in the **SCITT Time-Anchor Triple-Engine Verifier** repository is governed by the principles of [IETF RFC 7154 (*IETF Guidelines for Conduct*)](https://datatracker.ietf.org/doc/html/rfc7154) together with the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

We are committed to maintaining a respectful, harassment-free, vendor-neutral technical environment where all contributors—regardless of background, affiliation, or experience level—can collaborate on cryptographic specifications, interoperability testing, and formal verification.

## Expected Behavior

- **Respectful, Impersonal Technical Discourse (RFC 7154):** Critique specifications, protocols, proofs, and code—never individuals or organizations. Keep technical disagreements grounded in verifiable test vectors, RFC text, and reproducible evidence.
- **Vendor-Neutrality:** Treat all interoperable implementations fairly, clearly noting target draft revisions (`-03`, `-06`, `-08`, etc.) without commercial promotion or disparagement.
- **Constructive Collaboration:** Give and gracefully accept constructive code review, specification feedback, and security reports.
- **Inclusivity & Courtesy:** Use welcoming, professional language across issues, pull requests, discussions, and technical notes.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, intimidation, personal attacks, trolling, or insulting/derogatory comments.
- Public or private publishing of others' personal information (doxxing) without explicit permission.
- Sexualized language, unwelcome attention, or discriminatory remarks targeting any personal characteristic.
- Deliberate disruption of technical discussions, commercial spam, or bad-faith reporting.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies within all project spaces (GitHub issues, pull requests, discussions, commit messages, and technical documentation) and when representing the project in public standards forums.

## Reporting & Enforcement

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be reported privately to the project maintainer at **`audit@titancapitalforensics.com`**.

All reports will be reviewed promptly, investigated fairly, and handled with confidentiality to protect the reporter. Maintainers may clarify expectations, issue private warnings, remove offending comments, or restrict participation in response to conduct violations, proportional to severity and recurrence.

For security vulnerabilities (rather than conduct concerns), please follow [`SECURITY.md`](./SECURITY.md).
